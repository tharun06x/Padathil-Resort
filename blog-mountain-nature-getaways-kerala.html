Best Mountain & Nature Getaways in Kerala: Vattavada, Thattekad & Bhoothathankettu

Kerala is famous for its beaches and backwaters, but the state's mountains and forests offer equally beautiful destinations for a peaceful weekend getaway. From the cool hills of Vattavada to the forests of Thattekad and Bhoothathankettu, there are plenty of places to escape the noise of everyday life.

If you are searching for the best nature getaways in Kerala, these destinations are perfect for families, couples and groups of friends looking for fresh air, scenic landscapes and memorable experiences.

Vattavada: A Peaceful Mountain Escape

Located in the high ranges of Idukki, Vattavada is an excellent destination for travellers looking for cool weather, green hills and a quieter alternative to busy tourist centres.

The area is known for its beautiful mountain scenery, agricultural landscapes and peaceful surroundings. It is an ideal destination for a weekend trip where relaxation is just as important as sightseeing.

Things to Do in Vattavada

A visit to Vattavada can include:

  • Exploring scenic mountain roads
  • Enjoying sunrise and sunset views
  • Visiting local farms
  • Taking nature walks
  • Exploring nearby attractions
  • Spending quality time surrounded by nature

For couples and families, a comfortable stay surrounded by greenery can make the Vattavada experience even more relaxing.

Thattekad: A Paradise for Nature Lovers

If you enjoy forests, birds and wildlife, Thattekad should be on your Kerala travel list.

The region is well known for its rich biodiversity and natural surroundings. It is particularly appealing to birdwatchers and travellers who prefer outdoor experiences over crowded tourist attractions.

You can spend your day exploring the natural beauty of the region and return to your accommodation for a peaceful evening.

Bhoothathankettu: A Forest & Riverside Escape

Bhoothathankettu is another excellent option for a weekend getaway near Kochi.

Surrounded by greenery and natural landscapes, the area provides a refreshing break from city life. Its proximity to Thattekad also makes it possible to explore both destinations during the same trip.

Things to Do Around Bhoothathankettu

  • Explore the Bhoothathankettu area
  • Enjoy scenic countryside drives
  • Experience the surrounding forests
  • Visit nearby natural attractions
  • Combine your trip with Thattekad
  • Relax in a peaceful resort environment

Thattekad & Bhoothathankettu: A Perfect Weekend Escape

These two destinations work particularly well together for a short holiday.

You can arrive on the first day, settle into your resort and spend the evening relaxing. The next day can be dedicated to exploring Thattekad and Bhoothathankettu before returning home.

This makes the region an excellent choice for a 2-day Kerala nature getaway.

Which Nature Destination Should You Choose?

Choose Vattavada if you prefer:

  • Cool mountain weather
  • Hills and valleys
  • Scenic drives
  • Farm landscapes
  • A peaceful hill escape

Choose Thattekad if you prefer:

  • Birds and wildlife
  • Forests
  • Outdoor activities
  • Nature photography

Choose Bhoothathankettu if you want:

  • A convenient escape from Kochi
  • Forest and riverside scenery
  • A relaxed family trip
  • A combination of nature and sightseeing

Plan Your Kerala Nature Getaway

A great weekend does not need a complicated itinerary. Choose a beautiful destination, book a comfortable stay and leave enough time to simply enjoy the surroundings.

Whether you choose the mountains of Vattavada or the forests around Thattekad and Bhoothathankettu, Kerala offers plenty of opportunities to reconnect with nature.
