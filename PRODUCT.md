# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Couples, families, corporate groups, friends, celebration groups, and other travellers planning Kerala trips. They compare destination stays and make enquiries directly with the Padathil team, principally through WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

Padathil Stays is a family-run hospitality group offering private stays across Kerala. It helps guests find a welcoming base for time together, celebrations, and nature-led getaways.

## Positioning

Padathil Stays brings guests close to nature through its distinct settings: the Vattavada highlands, Vembanad Lake, and the Bhoothathankettu forest edge—while retaining the personal welcome of a family-run stay.

## Operating Context

Guests explore three properties—Nature Castle Resort Vattavada, Niva Waterways, and Riparian Ayur Resorts—and enquire with dates, guest count, and property preference through WhatsApp. The group is based in Thodupuzha, Idukki, Kerala, India.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The website provides property information, galleries, journal content, frequently asked questions, contact details, and WhatsApp enquiry links. Confirmed booking availability, pricing, and detailed property amenities must not be invented; enquiries are handled directly by the team.

## Brand Commitments

Use the established name “Padathil Stays” and the three property names. Preserve the family-run hospitality promise and the nature-first position. Existing logo assets are in `images/`.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains property imagery, logos, site copy, and contact details. The site identifies Padathil Stays as operating since 2012 and lists a WhatsApp-enabled contact number and reservations email. No independently verified testimonials, availability data, rates, or award claims are on hand.

## Product Principles

- Make choosing between the three nature settings easy.
- Make groups, celebrations, and shared trips feel welcome alongside couples and families.
- Keep the hospitality personal and direct.
- Let the connection to Kerala’s natural settings lead the product story.
- Do not imply availability, prices, or amenities that have not been confirmed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific accessibility requirement has been confirmed. Future interface work should retain the existing semantic navigation, skip link, and descriptive image alternatives, and should establish requirements before making access-related claims.
