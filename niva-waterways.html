A Private Lakefront Villa by Vembanad Lake

Wake up to the peaceful beauty of Kerala's backwaters. Niva Waterways is a premium four-bedroom villa facing Vembanad Lake, designed for families, friends, couples and groups looking for a private waterfront getaway.

Your Private Backwater Getaway

Imagine spending your holiday beside the calm waters of Vembanad Lake, surrounded by the peaceful charm of Kerala's backwater landscape.

Niva Waterways combines private villa accommodation with lake-facing views and recreational activities, making it an ideal choice for a relaxed group holiday or memorable family escape.
