Welcome to Padathil Stays

Family-Run Hospitality Group Across Kerala

Rooted in a family legacy and inspired by a deep connection with nature, Padathil Stays is a family-run hospitality group based in Thodupuzha, Idukki. Since 2012, we have brought warmth, personal attention and distinctive private villa experiences to guests across Kerala.

From mountain retreats to lakefront escapes and nature-filled destinations, our stays give families, couples, friends, or bachelor groups a comfortable place to slow down, reconnect and create lasting memories.

From Family Roots to Hospitality

Padathil Stays carries a legacy built on family, hard work, nature and community. Our roots go back generations to agriculture and paddy cultivation, a connection that inspired the name Padathil.

In 2012, Nature Castle Vattavada became the first step in our hospitality journey. Today, we continue to grow with one simple purpose: to create stays that feel welcoming, peaceful and memorable.

Padathil Stays is led by Managing Director Mr. Biju James, with Ms. Jisha Biju, Mr. Williams B. Padam and Ms. Merin B. Padam.
