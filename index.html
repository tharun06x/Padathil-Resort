Padathil Stays

Resort & Spa

One of the finest luxury resorts in Kumarakom, Padathil Stays is set beside the stunningly beautiful Kumarakom backwaters and is abundantly blessed with nature’s beauty — backwaters, coconut groves, exotic birds and serene sunsets. The resort offers a complement of 69 rooms, including Pool Villas and Lake View Villas that look out at the backwater in addition to a beautiful swimming pool. The resort’s picturesque outdoor areas make Padathil Stays the perfect choice for special events and memorable weddings.

Kumarakom

Situated besides the serene expanse of the Vembanad Lake, Kumarakom is renowned amongst travellers for the scenic beauty of its backwaters and a fabulously endowed bird sanctuary that is home to endemic and migratory species.

The backwaters of Kuttanad are beside the estuary where numerous rivers that flow from the mountains of Kerala drain into the Arabian Sea.

Guests will enjoy houseboat cruises along mysterious canals, partake of freshwater and saltwater fish and enjoy the warm hospitality that the people in the area are known to offer.
