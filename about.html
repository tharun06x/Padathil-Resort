About Padathil Stays

Family-run hospitality, distinctive Kerala destinations and private villa stays designed to feel welcoming, peaceful and memorable.

From Family Roots to Hospitality

Padathil Stays is a family-run hospitality company based in Thodupuzha, Idukki. Our roots go back generations to agriculture and paddy cultivation, a connection that inspired the name Padathil.

In 2012, Nature Castle Vattavada became the first step in our hospitality journey. Today, Padathil Stays brings the same warmth and personal care to a growing collection of private villa stays across Kerala.

Family-Run Hospitality

Family values are at the heart of every stay: warmth, attention and genuine care for guests.

Unique Destinations

Choose between Kerala's highlands, peaceful backwaters and lush nature landscapes.

Private Villa Experiences

Comfortable spaces for families, couples, friends and groups to reconnect.

Stay With Us. Feel At Home.

We want families to spend meaningful time together, friends to create new memories and travellers to experience the quieter side of Kerala.

Padathil Stays is led by Managing Director Mr. Biju James, with Ms. Jisha Biju, Mr. Williams B. Padam and Ms. Merin B. Padam.
