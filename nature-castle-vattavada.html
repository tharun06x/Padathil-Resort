Nature Castle Resort Vattavada

A Private Mountain Retreat in the Highlands of Kerala

Escape to the cool and peaceful landscapes of Vattavada. Nature Castle Resort is a rustic mountain retreat set on a forested hilltop, offering beautiful views of the surrounding highlands and Pambadum Shola Mountain. The property features wooden villa-style accommodation designed to complement its natural surroundings.

Your Peaceful Escape in Vattavada

Away from the rush of city life, Nature Castle offers a slower and more peaceful way to experience Kerala. Surrounded by greenery and mountain landscapes, the property is ideal for travellers looking for a quiet holiday, family getaway or refreshing mountain escape.

Its location also makes it a convenient base for exploring destinations around Vattavada and the Western Ghats, including Pampadum Shola National Park.
