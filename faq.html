Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before planning a stay with Padathil Stays.

What is Padathil Stays Group?

Padathil Stays is a family-run hospitality company based in Thodupuzha, Idukki, offering distinctive private villa stays across Kerala.

Where are the properties located?

Padathil Stays currently offers properties in Vattavada, the Vembanad Lake region near Cherthala, and Bhoothathankettu near Kothamangalam.

Which stay is best for a mountain holiday?

Nature Castle Resort Vattavada is ideal for travellers looking for a quiet mountain getaway surrounded by highland scenery.

Which stay is best for a backwater holiday?

Niva Waterways is a premium lake-facing villa near Vembanad Lake and is particularly suited to families, friends and groups.

Which stay is best for a nature getaway?

Riparian Ayur Resorts near Bhoothathankettu is a peaceful base for exploring natural surroundings and nearby attractions such as Thattekad.

Are the properties suitable for families and groups?

Yes. The collection is designed for families, couples and groups. Niva Waterways has a four-bedroom villa suited to larger groups.
