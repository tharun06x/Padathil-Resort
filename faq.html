Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before planning a stay with Padathil Stays.

What is Padathil Stays Group?

Padathil Stays is a family-run hospitality company based in Thodupuzha, Idukki, offering distinctive private villa stays across Kerala.

Where are the properties located?

The collection includes Vattavada in the highlands, the Vembanad Lake region near Cherthala, and Bhoothathankettu near Kothamangalam.

Which stay is right for my trip?

Nature Castle suits mountain breaks, Niva Waterways suits lake-facing family or group trips, and Riparian Ayur Resorts suits nature-focused stays. Tell us your dates and group size for a recommendation.

How do I check availability and book?

Use WhatsApp, call or email with your preferred property, check-in date, check-out date and guest count. Our reservations team will confirm the available options and next steps.

Can you help with meals, accessibility or group needs?

Yes. Include dietary needs, accessibility requirements, children and any group plans in your enquiry. Availability and arrangements vary by property.

Are the properties suitable for families and groups?

Yes. Padathil Stays offers private stays for families, couples, friends and groups. Niva Waterways has a four-bedroom villa suited to larger groups.
